# Licensing

Trade RL source code in revisions at and after the LGPL transition is licensed under `LGPL-3.0-or-later`, except where a file or bundled third-party material states a different license.

The canonical repository license text is `LICENSE`; SPDX-named copies are stored under `LICENSES/`. The GNU LGPL v3 incorporates the GNU GPL v3 terms, so the corresponding GPL text is bundled as well.

## Historical MIT revisions

Repository revisions published before the LGPL transition were distributed under the MIT License. Those historical MIT grants remain valid; the later LGPL transition does not revoke permissions already granted for those revisions. The historical MIT text is retained as `LICENSES/MIT.txt` for provenance and redistribution clarity.

## Dependencies and third-party material

Dependencies keep their own upstream licenses. `LICENSES/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` records notable runtime dependencies and attribution/trademark notes that require explicit visibility. A dependency being LGPL-compatible does not make it part of Trade RL's copyright ownership.

## Source availability

The preferred form for modification is this Git repository together with the lockfile, build configuration, tests, documentation, and source artifacts necessary to reproduce a distributed Trade RL build. Distribution packaging must preserve the applicable license files and third-party notices.

## Scope

This document records the project's licensing policy and engineering provenance. It is not legal advice. If a future contribution has uncertain copyright ownership or incompatible terms, distribution of the affected material must stop until the provenance is resolved.
